Every story here has been checked

Itihaas holds two kinds of stories. Gold standard verified ones, checked by a curator against evidence and context, and community contributed ones, clearly marked while they wait their turn. You always know which one you're hearing.

Where stories come from

Some arrive from locals, shopkeepers, families and visitors, in their own words. Others are sought out by our team at places where no one would think to submit on their own, an old caretaker, a retired groundskeeper, someone who has never been asked before.

Why listen instead of read

A place is easier to feel than to read about. Press play on any verified story and it's read back to you the way it was told, so you can keep walking, keep looking, and let the story meet you where you're standing.

On moderation

A story only carries the Verified seal once it's been recorded in the narrator's own voice. Until then it's marked Under moderation, the text has already been checked, but the voice recording is still pending, so you'll hear it read in English while that catches up.

On languages

Stories are being recorded in Kannada, Tamil and Telugu by their original narrators, not machine translated. Where a recording doesn't exist yet for the language you've picked, you'll hear the English narration instead, and we'll say so plainly rather than pretend otherwise.

Coverage, for now

Historic places lead the way, forts, ruins and monuments. Religious places, food places, and institutions and campuses are opening up alongside them.
